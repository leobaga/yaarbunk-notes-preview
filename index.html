About Bunk Budget

A small, free tool that answers the question students ask every week: can I skip this lecture?

What it does

Bunk Budget reads your attendance report and shows, for each subject, how many lectures you can miss and still stay above your college's cutoff. If you're already below, it shows how many to attend in a row to get back.

How it knows

It starts from the same sum you'd do by hand: lectures attended ÷ lectures held × 100. Then it does the part that's tedious by hand, working out the skips for every subject at once and keeping track as the semester goes on.

Who makes it

A student building the tools they wished they had. It's independent and not affiliated with any university.

Your data

  • Your screenshot is read by your own browser and never uploaded.
  • Your attendance numbers are saved only in that browser, on that device.
  • Your account holds only your email and password. No ads, no tracking.
